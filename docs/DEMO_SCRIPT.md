# 75-second demo script

Use synthetic data and keep the story focused on business value.

## 0–8 seconds — Hook

“A recovery automation can send messages all day, but the team still needs to know what is working and where money is being lost.”

Show the overview screen and the main recovered-revenue indicator.

## 8–25 seconds — Overview

“I built Central de Performance to turn payment and automation events into clear operational metrics. Here I can filter by period and recovery flow, then compare recovered revenue, conversions, active journeys, and email activity.”

Change one filter and let the cards and chart update.

## 25–43 seconds — Traceability

“The dashboard does not stop at the total. Every journey can be inspected from its original event through the recovery messages and the final outcome.”

Open one fictional journey and point to its timeline.

## 43–58 seconds — Operational quality

“It also identifies problems such as invalid checkout links, incorrect coupon rules, or journeys that need attention, so the team can act before the issue affects more customers.”

Open the alerts page and one synthetic example.

## 58–70 seconds — Engineering

“The application uses Next.js, TypeScript, Supabase, PostgreSQL, server-side business rules, authentication, and short-lived caching.”

Briefly show the architecture diagram or repository.

## 70–75 seconds — Close

“This is how I turn a complex automation into a system that people can understand, operate, and improve.”
